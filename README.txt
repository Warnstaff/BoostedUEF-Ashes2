Boosted UEF for Campaign mod
By Bubbles le Awesome

Installation:
-FIRST: Backup your "Assets\GameCore" folder in your main Ashes 2 directory!
Due to a lack of mod manager at the moment, this is important for restoring for non-mod use.

-SECOND: Copy the Gamecore folder from this zip file over to Assets\Gamecore and overwrite.
It only has one file.


What this mod does:
-Makes all Durantium costs set to 5 ore, and all applicable Elerite to 5 as well.
-All logistics costs are now 1 for buildable units.
-Meant for Campaign use. I imagine a rather miserable skirmish/multiplayer experience (though I have been wrong before).


Permissions:
As simple as this mod is, feel free to use it in your mods. No permissions needed.
